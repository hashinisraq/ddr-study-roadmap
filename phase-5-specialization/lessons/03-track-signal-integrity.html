Phase 5 · Track 3

Track: Signal Integrity / PCB / PHY

Make the physical electrical link work at multi-gigabit speeds - the deep version of Phase 0's signal-integrity intuition.

What you actually do

You keep the data eye open: model reflections, impedance, and crosstalk; apply equalization (like DDR5's DFE, Phase 2) to counter inter-symbol interference; and do channel modeling and de-embedding to separate the signal from the measurement setup.

What to learn

  • Bogatin's Signal and Power Integrity and Howard Johnson's High-Speed Digital Design - in full (Phase 0 only sampled them).
  • Transmission-line theory, termination, impedance control, crosstalk.
  • Eye diagrams, equalization (FFE/DFE), channel simulation; lab scopes and TDR.
Build to learn A concrete project: simulate a simple channel and watch the eye close as you raise the data rate, then reopen it with equalization. DDR5's DFE and DCA (Phase 2) become your daily tools here.
