Phase 2 · Lesson 6

Same-bank Refresh & Refresh Management (RFM)

DDR5 makes refresh hurt less, and adds a new command aimed squarely at the RowHammer problem.

Same-bank refresh (REFsb)

DDR4 mostly refreshed all banks together, freezing the whole chip for the refresh duration (tRFC). DDR5 can refresh one bank per bank group while the other banks keep serving requests.

Refresh Management (RFM) and RowHammer

RowHammer is a disturbance effect: hammering one row with many rapid activations can electrically flip bits in neighboring rows. DDR5's RFM is a new command, tied to how many activations have occurred, that gives the chip scheduled chances to shore up at-risk rows before they get disturbed.

Two goals, one area Same-bank refresh is about performance (refresh steals less time). RFM is about reliability/security (defending against RowHammer). Both are refinements of the refresh machinery you learned in Phase 1.
