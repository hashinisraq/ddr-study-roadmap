Phase 2 · Lesson 11

Command/Address Changes & New Training

The faster, two-sub-channel link needs a reworked command/address scheme and more tuning at boot than DDR4.

A reworked CA bus

DDR5 changed how commands and addresses are encoded on the bus, and each sub-channel has its own CA (Lesson 1). The command set does the same jobs you know from Phase 1 (activate, read, write, precharge, refresh), but the encoding and timing were redesigned for DDR5's speed and structure.

More training steps

Recall DDR4 training (Phase 1, Lesson 7): ZQ calibration, write leveling, read/DQS gate training, Vref training. DDR5 adds more, because its margins are tighter:

  • CA training - tune the command/address link timing.
  • CS training - tune chip-select timing.
  • Refined read/write leveling and the duty-cycle adjust (Lesson 8).
Where DDR5 firmware diverges most If you take the firmware / training track in Phase 5, this is where DDR5 departs furthest from DDR4: more training steps, per-sub-channel tuning, and a larger register space to drive it all. The goal is the same - center every sampling point in its eye - there are just more knobs.
