Phase 1 · Lesson 12

Module Types: UDIMM, RDIMM, LRDIMM

Same DRAM chips, different module designs - the difference is how much buffering sits between the controller and the chips.

TypeBuffersTypical use
UDIMMNothing - directDesktops
SO-DIMMNothing (small form factor)Laptops, mini-PCs
RDIMMCommand/address (register)Servers
LRDIMMCommand/address and dataHigh-capacity servers
ECC, separately ECC (error-correcting) modules add a 9th chip - 72 bits instead of 64 - to detect and correct errors. ECC is about reliability and is independent of the UDIMM/RDIMM/ LRDIMM buffering choice (though servers usually combine ECC + RDIMM/LRDIMM).
Check yourself
  1. What does an RDIMM buffer that a UDIMM doesn't?
  2. What extra does an LRDIMM buffer beyond an RDIMM, and what does that enable?
  3. Is ECC the same thing as registering? Explain.
