Initialization & Training

You can't just power on and read. At DDR speeds the link must be tuned to the board before it works - this is the world of DDR firmware.

The power-up sequence

Voltages and the RESET pin must come up in a defined order, after which the controller configures the chip's mode registers. Only then can training begin.

ZQ calibration

The chip calibrates its output-driver strength and ODT resistance against a precise external 240 Ω reference resistor (RZQ), so its impedances are correct despite process/temperature variation.

Write leveling - the fly-by problem

DDR4 routes the clock in a fly-by line that passes each chip in turn, so the clock arrives at each chip at a slightly different time. Write leveling measures that skew and delays each chip's data strobe (DQS) to re-align it with the clock at that chip.

Read training & Vref training

Read (DQS gate) training tunes exactly when to sample incoming read data; DQ Vref training places the voltage threshold (Phase 0, Lesson 6) in the center of the data "eye" so 0s and 1s are read correctly.

Why this is a whole career "Training" is a large part of real DDR work: the firmware/MRC track in Phase 5 lives here. DDR5 adds even more training (CA training, duty-cycle adjust) because its margins are tighter.
