The Bank State Machine (the grammar)

Each bank is always in a state, and commands move it between states. This is the grammar that says which commands are legal when.

The two rules that matter

  • You can only RD/WR a bank that is ACTIVE (a row is open). You cannot read a closed row.
  • You can only ACTIVATE a bank that is IDLE. To switch to a different row you must PRECHARGE first - you cannot open a row on top of another.
Why every timing hangs off this Each arrow has a minimum time attached (next lesson): ACT→RD waits tRCD, PRE→ACT waits tRP, and a row must stay ACTIVE at least tRAS. The state machine tells you which move is legal; the timings tell you how long before the next move.
Check yourself
  1. From IDLE, what command must come first, and why can't you RD immediately?
  2. You have row 5 open but need row 9 in the same bank. What sequence gets you there?
  3. Why can different banks be in different states at the same time?
